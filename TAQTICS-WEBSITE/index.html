Where Taqtics Fits

Restaurant Management Software vs POS and Back-Office Systems

Restaurant technology serves different parts of the operating model. Taqtics focuses on restaurant operations execution —the work teams need to complete consistently across locations.

Taqtics can operate alongside the POS, ERP, inventory, HR, or other systems a restaurant already uses rather than replacing every part of the technology stack.

POS systems

Primarily manage orders, transactions, payments, and sales data.

Inventory systems

Primarily manage stock, ingredients, purchasing, and inventory records.

ERP / HR systems

Manage broader finance, people, payroll, procurement, or enterprise processes.

Taqtics

Manages daily tasks, SOP execution, audits, inspections, asset tracking, corrective actions, and multi-location operational visibility.
